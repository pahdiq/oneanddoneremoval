Talk about your pickup
How much is junk removal in Provo?

Removal starts at $49. That is a starting price, not a price for every item or load. Send photos of everything you want removed and describe the access. We confirm your final quote before you agree to a pickup.

Can I ask about just one couch or another bulky item?

Yes. Send a photo and approximate dimensions of the piece. For a couch, table, or bed frame, mention narrow doors, stairs, and whether it needs to come apart. We’ll discuss the item and confirm whether we can take it before scheduling.

Can you help me plan an apartment move-out?

Tell us what needs to go, your Provo address, and your move-out deadline. Include the floor, elevator access, and any loading or parking rules. Keep your belongings separate from roommates’ items and identify exactly what you want removed. Timing and scope are confirmed directly.

Is a photo really required for every job?

Yes. A photo is required for every job, including single-item pickups. Start with a wide shot of the full pile and add closer photos of anything hidden or unusually large. Tell us if the pile changes after you receive a quote.

Do you pick up paint or hazardous waste?

No. We do not take any paint, including lead-based paint, or any hazardous materials. Keep those items separate. A garage cleanout does not include everything stored in a garage; ask us to confirm acceptance of other items before booking.

Do you serve Orem too?

Yes. Our service corridor includes Provo, Orem, Lindon, Pleasant Grove, American Fork, and Lehi, continuing north to Ogden. Send your address so we can confirm the location and pickup availability.
