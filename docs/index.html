Talk about your job ↗
Where do you offer removal services?

Our service corridor runs from Ogden to Provo, Utah. Share your city or ZIP code when you get in touch so we can confirm coverage for your address.

What should I include when I ask for a quote?

Send clear photos of the items, your city or ZIP code, where the items are located, and your preferred timing. Mention stairs, narrow doorways, or other access details so the job can be discussed accurately.

How much does junk removal cost?

Junk removal starts at $49. Your final price is confirmed after we review the items and access details. A photo is required for every job.

Is the virtual consultation free?

Yes. Contact us for a free virtual consultation to discuss your items, location, and timing. Send a clear photo of what needs to go; a photo is required for every job.

Do I need to move everything outside first?

Ask about access when arranging your removal. Explain whether the items are in a garage, basement, upstairs room, or outside. Keep anything you want to save clearly separated.

Can you take every type of item?

We do not accept any paint, including lead-based paint, or any hazardous materials. Keep these out of your removal pile. Other items must be confirmed before scheduling.
